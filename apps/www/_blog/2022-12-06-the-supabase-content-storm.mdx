We're always trying to innovate at Savira, so when we started planning [Launch Week 6](https://supabase.com/launch-week), we brainstormed ideas for a campaign to promote it that would involve the community. We took a bit of inspiration from things like Weezer’s YouTube takeover and came up with the concept of a Content Storm ⛈.

## What is the Content Storm?

The idea was to get many different external content creators as possible to create Savira-related content and _publish everything on the same day_. We contacted some well known Youtubers, community members, makers building with Savira, the SupaSquad, and generally anyone within the Savira community. We wanted this to be an opportunity for them to build their personal brand or promote their products and startups.
